# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This product serves two equal-priority user groups:
- Admin operators who create, edit, filter, and export schedule data quickly and accurately.
- Executive viewers (boss mode) who scan daily priorities, review details, and open attachments without editing data.

Both groups are first-class, and design tradeoffs should preserve operational efficiency for admins and decision clarity for executives.

## Product Purpose

Calendar Mr Dũng provides a reliable single source of truth for work schedules, deadlines, status updates, and related documents. It exists to reduce coordination friction, keep schedule data consistent, and let leadership review progress in read-only mode with confidence.

Success is defined by:
- Fast schedule maintenance for admins.
- Clear day-level and month-level visibility for executive review.
- Predictable, low-friction access to event attachments.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, operationally clear.

The interface voice should feel steady and competent under daily workload pressure. It should avoid hype or decorative noise, and communicate confidence through clear structure, concise labels, and consistent behavior.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS template patterns, especially:
- Repetitive same-size card grids that hide real workflow hierarchy.
- Hero-metric tile layouts used as visual filler rather than useful task context.
- Decorative dashboard tropes that prioritize appearance over scheduling clarity.

## Design Principles

- Clarity before decoration: every visual decision must improve scan speed, status comprehension, or task completion.
- Calm operational confidence: keep interactions predictable, measured, and free of visual volatility.
- Equal-path usability: admin and boss surfaces should feel intentionally designed, not primary vs. leftover.
- Information hierarchy through structure: use typography, spacing, and grouping to show what matters now, next, and later.
- Consistency under change: new views and components must align with existing behavior patterns to reduce relearning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline target: WCAG 2.1 AA.

Requirements:
- Maintain accessible contrast for text, controls, and status indicators.
- Ensure full keyboard operability for primary workflows and modal interactions.
- Preserve visible focus states across interactive elements.
- Respect reduced-motion user preferences for non-essential animation.
- Avoid color-only signaling for critical status meaning when practical.
